Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

SanjanaIAS is an independent educational platform. We take your privacy seriously.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your username, a securely hashed password, and a security question/answer (for password recovery). We also store your study preferences, including bookmarks, notes, Mains drafts, and quiz progress.

How we store it

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, a PostgreSQL database provider. We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only you can access your personal study data.

How to delete your data

You can permanently delete your bookmarks, notes, drafts, and progress at any time by visiting the My Library → Data tab and clicking "Erase all local data".
